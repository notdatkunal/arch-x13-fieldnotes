TL;DR Workload Matrix

Workload Rating Practical Notes
Web, Backend & CLI Dev Great Fast NVMe, 15 GiB RAM, Docker, and low-overhead Wayland tiling.
AI-Assisted / Cloud Coding Great Portable ThinkPad form factor paired with lightweight terminals/editors and cloud agents.
Coding & Tutorial Live Streaming Good Smooth at 1080p30 or 720p60 in OBS using VA-API H.264 hardware encoding on AC power.
Light 1080p Video Editing Good Works well for cuts, screen recordings, and voiceovers (enable proxy clips in Kdenlive/Shotcut for complex timelines).
Audio / Podcasts / Thumbnails Great Native PipeWire + WirePlumber routing; Audacity, GIMP, and Inkscape run smoothly.
3D Gaming / Heavy GPU Rendering Limited Fine for 2D/indie/older titles (vulkan-intel), not suited for AAA 3D games or heavy Blender GPU rendering.
Local LLMs / ML Training Not Ideal No dedicated GPU/VRAM or CUDA; only tiny CPU-quantized models are practical locally.
