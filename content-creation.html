Overview Why Arch + Hyprland Hardware & Dev Streaming & Editing

Content Creation, Live Streaming & Light Video Editing

For coding tutorials, screen-recorded demos, non-gaming live streams, and 1080p video editing, this setup works surprisingly well—provided you offload encoding to Intel's hardware VA-API / Quick Sync engine.

1. Already-Installed Wayland Capture Stack

  • Screen & Window Portals: xdg-desktop-portal-hyprland (1.5.0-1) enables native screen and window sharing on Wayland.
  • Audio & Video Routing: pipewire (1:1.6.11-2) and wireplumber (0.5.19-1) route mic and desktop audio with low latency.
  • Built-in Capture Tools: wf-recorder (0.6.0-2), grim, slurp, hyprshot, swappy, v4l-utils, and ffmpeg are already present (with webcam devices /dev/video0 and /dev/video1 detected).
  • Hardware Acceleration: intel-media-driver (26.3.5-1) gives ffmpeg hardware encoders including h264_vaapi, hevc_vaapi, h264_qsv, hevc_qsv, and vp9_vaapi.

2. Light Video Editing (1080p)

Works Well For

1080p screen recordings, coding walkthroughs, talking-head YouTube videos, trimming/cuts, voiceover mixing, text overlays, and simple transitions (Kdenlive, Shotcut, LosslessCut, ffmpeg).

Limits & Workarounds

4K timelines, heavy color grading, or stacked blur effects will lag during scrubbing. Enable proxy clips in Kdenlive/Shotcut for smooth editing, and watch free space on the 476.9 GiB NVMe drive.

3. Live Streaming (OBS Studio on Hyprland)

Realistic Target: Great for coding streams, browser/slide demos, podcasts, and 2D/lightweight games at 1080p30 or 720p60 (4500–6000 kbps). Not suitable for playing demanding 3D games while live-encoding on the same integrated GPU.
  1. Use VA-API H.264 in OBS (Avoid Software x264): The i5-10310U is a 4-core ultrabook chip. Software x264 alongside OBS scenes, an IDE, and a browser causes thermal throttling; FFmpeg VAAPI H.264 keeps CPU load low.
  2. Stream on AC Power: On battery, intel_pstate defaults to powersave and tighter thermal/power limits. Plug in AC power with unobstructed airflow for sustained streams.
  3. Capture a Monitor or Dedicated Workspace: Because Hyprland tiles and resizes windows dynamically, capturing a full Monitor/Display (or dedicating a Hyprland workspace to your stream) prevents aspect-ratio jumps when opening terminal splits.
  4. Skip Heavy Webcam AI Blur: Real-time background removal plugins eat CPU/iGPU headroom quickly; prefer good lighting and physical framing.

4. Audio, Thumbnails & Quick Clips

  • Voiceovers & Podcasts: pipewire + wireplumber paired with Audacity or Tenacity uses minimal CPU.
  • Thumbnails & Markup: swappy (already installed) handles quick screenshot annotations; GIMP and Inkscape run smoothly.
  • Instant Screen Clips: Use wf-recorder + slurp directly from Hyprland for lightweight region recordings.

5. Starter Packages to Install

These tools are not installed by default yet and can be added whenever you're ready:

# Streaming, video editing, and VA-API diagnostics
sudo pacman -S obs-studio kdenlive libva-utils

# Optional: instant lossless cuts (AUR) and audio editing
yay -S losslesscut-bin
sudo pacman -S audacity

After installing libva-utils, check active hardware encode/decode profiles with:

vainfo
