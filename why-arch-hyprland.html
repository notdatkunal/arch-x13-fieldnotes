Why Arch Linux + Hyprland (illogical-impulse)

Arch's rolling-release model matches Hyprland's fast development cadence, while Hyprland hits a sweet spot between keyboard-first tiling efficiency and modern Wayland polish.

1. Why Arch + Hyprland Together?

Rolling, Bleeding-Edge Updates

Hyprland evolves rapidly with Wayland protocol updates and GPU/driver fixes. Arch ships the latest kernel, mesa, intel-media-driver, and Hyprland immediately without waiting on fixed distro cycles.

AUR Companion Ecosystem

Hyprland setups rely on many modular tools (widget shells, wallpaper daemons, launchers, screen capture helpers). Nearly everything is packaged in official Arch repos or the AUR.

Minimal Base

Arch doesn't force a full desktop environment (GNOME/KDE) underneath, avoiding background service bloat and session conflicts.

2. Why This Specific Setup (end-4/dots-hyprland + uwsm)?

  • Quickshell-Powered Desktop UX: Instead of piecing together a bare bar manually, illogical-impulse uses Quickshell (illogical-impulse-quickshell-git) to deliver integrated widgets, media/system controls, notifications, and polished animations out of the box.
  • uwsm Systemd Integration: Launching Hyprland 0.57.2 under uwsm (0.27.2) cleanly manages environment variables, XDG portals, and user systemd units—preventing common Wayland headaches with screensharing and app launches.
  • Low Idle Footprint: Baseline memory usage stays around ~3.3 GiB out of 15 GiB, leaving plenty of headroom for editors, containers, and browsers.

3. How Hyprland Compares to Other Setups

Setup Key Difference vs. Hyprland
X11 Tiling WMs (i3, bspwm, dwm) Hyprland runs natively on Wayland (no screen tearing, proper per-monitor fractional scaling and mixed refresh rates) and builds blur, animations, and rounded corners right into the compositor without needing picom.
Sway (Wayland) Sway aims to be a minimal, static i3 drop-in on Wayland. Hyprland prioritizes dynamic tiling, smooth animations, plugins, and visual customization.
Full DEs (GNOME, KDE Plasma) Hyprland uses less RAM/CPU and centers on keyboard-driven window management configured via readable plain-text files (hyprland.conf).

4. Trade-offs & Maintenance Tips

Keep dotfiles synced before big upgrades: Because both Arch and Hyprland move fast, major updates occasionally change config syntax. Pull/check upstream changes in end-4/dots-hyprland when running large pacman/yay upgrades so Quickshell widgets and keybinds stay aligned.
